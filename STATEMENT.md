# Problem Statement

Small savings groups, cooperative societies, and student-run finance clubs often track members' deposits and withdrawals in a paper ledger or a plain spreadsheet. This makes it easy to lose track of a member's current balance, miscount a withdrawal, or let someone withdraw more money than they actually have.

## Scope
This project implements a lightweight, console-based bank account system covering account creation, deposits, withdrawals, and balance and detail lookups. It focuses on core account management rather than real banking features such as interest calculation, multiple currencies, inter-account transfers, or persistent storage across sessions.

## Target Users
- Small savings groups or cooperative societies
- Students learning core programming concepts through a realistic, relatable example

## High-Level Features
- Account creation with a validated name, account number, and PIN
- Deposits and withdrawals, with PIN verification and balance checks
- Balance inquiry and full account detail lookup
- A complete list of all accounts in the system
